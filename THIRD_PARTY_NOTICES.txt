The SiouxFalls_net.tntp, SiouxFalls_node.tntp and SiouxFalls_trips.tntp
files are downloaded from upstream and are not redistributed here. They are NOT relicensed under this repository's
MIT software licence. The upstream repository restricts its donated datasets
to academic research, requires acknowledgement of the source in publications,
and places responsibility for derived results on data users. Please consult
and comply with the linked upstream terms. The processed Sioux Falls CSVs
retain the same source attribution and do not remove those restrictions.

The original network and demand are attributed upstream to LeBlanc, Morlok
and Pierskalla (1975), "An efficient approach to solving the road network
equilibrium traffic assignment problem". Coordinates are artificial benchmark
coordinates. They are not a georeferenced road inventory. Free-flow time units
are 0.01 hours, and the benchmark demands follow the scaling documented by
the upstream repository.
